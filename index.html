YouTube Tools

YouTube Tools is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Japan Ticker (daily Japan money and markets news in English) and Japan Insight (practical guides to living and working in Japan).

It runs on the owner's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channels' own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, translations, thumbnails, captions and playlists. It is not offered to the public.
