Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

YouTube Tools ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Japan Ticker and Japan Insight. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to access the owner's own YouTube channels: channel information, the channels' videos and playlists, and the ability to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, translations, thumbnails and captions.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos. The OAuth access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it does not access data of any other user.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Revoking access stops the app from accessing the channels, and the stored tokens become invalid.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact information on the Japan Ticker or Japan Insight YouTube channel pages.
